REACH Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

REACH is a private communication app for REACH ID based chat, contact requests, and voice calling. This Privacy Policy explains what information REACH collects, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: information used to create or sign in to an account, such as Google sign-in details, phone or email information, and authentication identifiers.
  • Profile information: your REACH ID, display name, profile details, and account status information.
  • Contacts and requests: contact connections, contact request status, blocked users, and related relationship information needed to run the app.
  • Messages and call information: direct messages, message delivery status, voice call status, timestamps, duration, and call history metadata needed to provide chat and calling features.
  • Device and app information: app version, device type, notification token, diagnostics, crash information, and technical logs used to keep the service secure and reliable.

How We Use Information

  • To create and manage REACH accounts.
  • To help users find contacts by REACH ID and manage contact requests.
  • To deliver messages, call notifications, and voice calling features.
  • To improve reliability, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and protect users.
  • To comply with legal, safety, and platform requirements.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate REACH, including authentication, cloud hosting, database, notification, and communication infrastructure providers. Information may also be shared if required by law, to protect users, or to prevent fraud or abuse.

Messages and Call History

REACH uses messages and call records to provide chat and voice calling features. Some web messages may be automatically removed from the web experience after a limited time. App users may retain chat history depending on app behavior and user actions.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

User Choices

Users can manage their account, contacts, requests, and profile information inside the app where available. Users may also request account or data deletion by contacting us.

Children

REACH is not intended for children. Users should only use REACH if they are old enough to consent to the processing of their personal information under applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at reach.safechat@gmail.com.
