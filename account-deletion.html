Delete Your REACH Account

Last updated: June 13, 2026

You can request deletion of your REACH account and associated data by contacting REACH support from the email address linked to your account.

Request account deletion

What to Include

  • Your REACH ID.
  • The email address or phone number linked to your account.
  • A clear request to delete your REACH account.

What Will Be Deleted

After verifying the request, we will delete or anonymize account information associated with your REACH account, including profile details, REACH ID account records, contact relationships, contact requests, and stored chat or call records controlled by REACH where deletion is technically available.

What May Be Retained

Some information may be retained for a limited time where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, backup recovery, or dispute resolution. Web messages may already be automatically removed after a limited time as described in the app.

Processing Time

We aim to process verified deletion requests within a reasonable time after receiving enough information to identify the account.

Contact

For account deletion or privacy questions, contact us at reach.safechat@gmail.com.
